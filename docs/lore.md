# Termablo lore: The Stolen Ember

This is the world bible. It explains why Emberhold is the last lit town,
why the dark hides what lives in it, why every portal leads home, and who
the bosses were before they were bosses. The game's text (zone lore,
NPC lines, unique flavor, boss names) should agree with this file. Where
it does not yet, the rebase list at the end says what to change.

## The story as Emberhold tells it

Before there was a town there was only the Dark, and the Dark was not
empty. It was the thing that lives below, and above was only the thin
place it had not reached yet.

A king came walking out of the west with his people behind him and
nowhere left to go. His name was Edran. He went down into the earth
alone, further than anyone had, until he found the place where the rock
itself burns. He took a single coal from it, carried it up in his bare
hands, and set it down on a hill. That hill is Emberhold. The coal is
the Ember, and it has not gone out since.

The Dark cannot cross light that was taken from it. That is the whole
reason the town stands. Every lantern in Emberhold is lit from the
forge, and the forge is lit from the Ember, and so the Dark stops at the
gate.

But a stolen thing is a called-for thing. Everything that lives below
has been climbing toward the Ember ever since, slowly, the way water
finds a crack. The Fallen were first, because they are small. Then the
dead, because the Ember's warmth wakes them. Then the Cult, because some
in the town decided the Dark was owed. And beneath them all, the fire
the Ember was cut from, which wants its piece back.

King Edran paid for the coal. The Dark took his face for it, and then
his skull, and he ruled Emberhold for twenty more years with a hollow
crown on a head that was not there. When he died they laid him in the
crypt in the eastern hills with his court around him, and the braziers
they lit for his vigil have lit themselves at every dusk since. He sits
his throne of ribs there still, and he is not finished being king.

His Oracle, who had told him not to take the coal, walked north into
Blackmarsh and into the water beneath it and did not come up. She did
this on purpose. Her cold light still drifts over the marsh, and the
grotto beneath it hums with it, and the lost still follow it down.

That is the town's story. Children know it. Nobody says it loudly.

## What is actually true

The tale is right about the shape of things and wrong about the moral.

**The Ember is not a defense. It is a wound.** Edran did not steal a
coal from a pit of fire, he tore a piece out of something that was
whole. The Dark that presses on Emberhold is not malice, it is the
pressure of a thing trying to close. Every monster that climbs toward
the town is a clot moving toward the cut. This is why the deeper zones
are hotter: the player is walking down the wound toward where it bleeds.

**The Oracle did not flee. She is the dam.** She saw that the fire below
would rise through the ground to reach the Ember and burn the town from
underneath. Cold water on the wound. She drowned herself in the
grotto's pool so that her cold light would sit between the Abyss and
the surface, and it has held for generations. The lost she calls into
the water are the price of holding it. Every drowned soul is another
hand on the dam. When the player kills her, the Abyss opens. Voss says
so: "Beneath her pool, the rock burns." The town sleeps soundly for the
first time and has no idea what it has lost.

**The Bone King is not the villain. He is the first casualty.** The
Dark took his face and skull as the price, and what sits the throne of
ribs is what a man becomes when he keeps a bargain with the Dark for
too long. His vigil braziers light themselves because the Ember's fire
still answers to the hand that carried it. His crypt is the town's own
royal crypt; the Skeletons are his court; the Wraiths are his honor
guard who swore to follow him into the Dark and did.

**The Wanderer owes the Ember nothing.** Every person in Emberhold was
born under its light and cannot think straight about it. The player
walked in from outside. That is why Voss hires them, and that is why
the ending is theirs to decide. See Open threads.

## Two kinds of light

The game's core rule is that you see only what the light reaches. The
lore gives that two faces.

**Ember-light** is warm: torches, braziers, lanterns, the forge, fire
spells, Fire Imps, Hellspawn, lava. It came from below. The Dark cannot
cross it but is drawn to it. Where the player carries a torch, the
player is a beacon.

**Cold light** is the Oracle's: the drifting marsh lights, the Grotto's
crystals, Will-o'-Wisps, Wraiths, Mirela's crystal, the town fountain's
water. It holds the Dark down rather than back. It is light without
heat, and nothing that came from the Abyss can stand in it for long,
which is why the Grotto is where the fire stops and the water begins.

**The Dark** is not an absence. It is the body of the thing below,
reaching. Monsters are invisible in it because they are inside it. The
"far beyond you" warning on a too-deep zone is the Dark noticing.

**Portals** open onto Emberhold's fountain square because every spark of
the Ember knows the way back to the hearth. A Scroll of Town Portal is a
pinch of forge ash folded in paper.

**Altars** are Edran's. He raised them on the way up as he carried the
coal, a drop of his own blood on each to buy another hour of safe
passage. Their "blood-light" restores the player because the bargain
still pays out, once, to whoever bleeds on them.

## Places

**Emberhold.** The hill Edran set the coal on. The forge is the Ember's
hearth, the lanterns are lit from it, the fountain in the square is fed
by a thread of the Oracle's water that runs under the hills. The town
lives between the fire and the water and knows neither well.

**The Ashen Fields.** Where the Ember first touched the surface. Edran
set it down to rest on the way to the hill and it burned the forest
flat. The ash never fully left; the moonlight on it is the only daylight
the game has. The Fallen camp here because it is the first warm ground
they reach climbing up. The crypt entrance ruin on the eastern edge was
the chapel built over Edran's tomb.

**The Crypt of the Fallen.** Emberhold's royal crypt, four floors, the
Bone King on the fourth. "Of the Fallen" meant the honored dead when it
was carved. It means something else now. Cold, dust, and vigil braziers
that nobody tends.

**Blackmarsh.** North of the fields, where the Oracle walked into the
water. The standing stones with crystals are hers: she raised them as
she went, the way Edran raised altars, and they are the top of the dam.
The cold lights over the water are her calling the lost down to hold
it. Do not follow them. Everyone follows them.

**The Sunken Grotto.** Below the marsh, the Oracle's pool, three floors.
Crystals grown from her cold light, Grotto Spiders that nest in the
dark between them, Crystal Golems that are her light given a shape to
guard with. The Drowned Dead are the lost she has taken. She dissolves
into mist and reforms because she is not a body anymore, she is the
water itself with a woman's memory of a face.

**The Burning Abyss.** Beneath the pool. The place Edran cut the coal
from, still open, still hot. "The Abyss has no floor" is literally true
in the lore: the wound goes down as far as the thing it was cut from.
Imps, Hellspawn, Cultists who made it this far, and whatever the Ember
was cut from, which has not been named yet. See Open threads.

## People

**Hadrik the Smith.** Keeper of the forge, which is to say keeper of the
Ember, which is to say the most important person in Emberhold and the
only one who knows it. The keeping passes hand to hand. He has held it
forty years. "The day it goes out, we run" is not a figure of speech.
He sells steel because steel is what you can make with a fire that
never cools, and because somebody should be armed.

**Old Mirela.** Alchemist, healer. Her crystal was dug out of the Grotto
and sings when the Oracle dreams, which makes her the one person in town
in contact with the dam. She does not know what the Oracle is holding
back but she knows the Oracle is holding something. Her potions are cut
with fountain water. Her healing is the Oracle's, secondhand.

**Captain Voss.** Captain of a guard that has no king to serve. His
father went down into the crypt to end the Bone King with Kingsbane
and did not come back. The heirloom he gives the player after the crypt
quest is his father's, recovered or kept, it does not matter which.
Voss believes the town's story completely. He is wrong about the Oracle
and will never know.

**The villagers.** Born under the Ember. They cannot imagine a town
without it or a dark without hunger. Their lines are rumor, and most of
the rumors are true.

**The Wanderer.** The player. Came from outside, from somewhere the
Dark has not reached or has already won. Owes the Ember nothing. The
only person who can carry it, and the only one who could choose to.

## Factions and monsters

**The Fallen and their Shamans.** The smallest things in the Dark, which
is why they got through the cracks first. Red because they have been
climbing toward fire their whole existence. Shamans hurl fire because
they have eaten sparks from the Ashen Fields. They camp in the open
because they are too stupid to be afraid of the moon.

**The dead.** Zombies, Skeletons, Archers, Ghouls, Wraiths. The Ember's
warmth seeps into the ground and wakes what the ground holds. In the
crypt they are Edran's court and still obey him. Elsewhere they are
simply awake and angry about it.

**The Drowned.** The Oracle's. The Drowned Dead are the lost she called
into the water; the Will-o'-Wisps are her cold light with nothing left
to be attached to. They are not evil. They are a dam made of people.

**The Ember Cult.** Emberhold's own. A generation ago a faction decided
the Dark was owed its coal and the town's suffering was the debt
collecting. They went down to carry the Ember's fire home, got as far
as the Abyss, and the fire remade them. Ember Cultists cast fire; they
are what a townsperson looks like after the Abyss agrees with them.
Future hook: their founder is a name a villager could drop.

**The Abyss-born.** Fire Imps and Hellspawn. The wound's own blood,
climbing. Crystal Golems are not theirs: those are the Oracle's, and the
two fight where they meet.

## Bosses

**The Bone King.** King Edran. The Hollow Crown is his, and so is the
line "the king it belonged to did not need a skull." Killing him does not
end the Dark's climb, it only ends his part in it, which is why the
quest line says "the crypt bells may ring again" and nothing larger.

**The Drowned Oracle.** The dam. Her name is not recorded; "Oracle" was
her office. The Crown of the Drowned is hers, dripping. Killing her
opens the Abyss. The game treats this as progress. The lore treats it as
the moment the Wanderer becomes responsible.

**The Last Wanderer.** The stranger Emberhold hired before you. He went
down past the Oracle's pool and came back up carrying the Abyss's fire:
what the Ember was cut from, wearing a person. He looks like you, an `@`
with a red glow, and every other light goes out near him. When you
portal home, he follows, puts out Emberhold's lanterns and hunts its
people on his way to the forge. Spec: `prompts/last-wanderer.md`.

## Named things

How the existing uniques fit. Flavor lines in quotes are the ones already
in the game and should stay.

- **Wanderer's Shroud.** The player's own history. "Worn by one who never
  stopped walking."
- **Shard of Night.** A sliver of the Dark that was cut when the Ember was.
  "It drinks the torchlight."
- **Hollow Crown.** Edran's. "The king it belonged to did not need a skull."
- **Emberbrand.** Forged by a previous keeper from the forge's own fire.
  "Its edge never cools."
- **The Last Lantern.** The lantern Edran carried the coal in, or a copy
  so old nobody can tell. "It burns when all else is dark."
- **Moonstride.** Made for walking the Ashen Fields, where moonlight is the
  only safe light.
- **Band of Ashes.** A ring of cooled Ember-slag from the forge floor.
  "Warm to the touch, always."
- **Gravewarden's Coat.** The crypt had wardens once, before the braziers
  started lighting themselves. "Stitched from the shrouds of the unquiet."
- **Eye of the Deep.** A crystal from the Grotto that still watches for the
  Oracle. "It blinks when you are not looking."
- **Crown of the Drowned.** The Oracle's. "Water still drips from its rim."
- **Bloodfist.** A Cultist's gauntlets. "Never clean. Never dry."
- **Kingsbane.** Voss's father's axe. "Seven crowns have rolled beneath it."
  Edran was not the first king to come looking for a hearth; the axe is
  older than the town. Room here for the Seven Crowns idea later.
- **Starfall Plate.** The one object in the game that came from above, not
  below. "Forged from a fallen star; it remembers the sky." Leave it as a
  mystery; it is the hook for a sky-side story if one is ever wanted.

## Timeline

1. **The Dark.** Everything below. Nothing above worth naming.
2. **The Theft.** Edran goes down alone, cuts the coal, carries it up.
   Raises the altars on the way. Sets it down in a forest, which becomes
   the Ashen Fields. Carries it to the hill.
3. **The Founding.** Emberhold. The forge is built around the Ember. The
   lanterns are lit. The Dark stops at the gate.
4. **The Price.** The Dark takes Edran's face, then his skull. He rules
   twenty more years in a hollow crown. His Oracle tells him to return the
   coal. He refuses.
5. **The Drowning.** The Oracle walks north into Blackmarsh and down into
   the Grotto's pool. Raises the standing stones on the way. The cold
   light begins. The lost begin to follow it.
6. **The Vigil.** Edran dies. Laid in the crypt with his court. The
   braziers light themselves the first dusk after and every dusk since.
   The Gravewardens keep the crypt for a time, then stop.
7. **The Schism.** A generation ago. The Ember Cult forms, goes down,
   does not come back as itself.
8. **The Captain's father.** Goes into the crypt with Kingsbane. Does not
   come back.
9. **Now.** Hadrik has kept the forge forty years. The Fallen camp in the
   fields. Voss has no one left to send. A Wanderer walks in from the west.

## Open threads

These are decisions not yet made. They do not block rebasing the
existing text.

- **What the Ember was cut from.** Decided on 2026-10-03: it wears the
  Last Wanderer. See Bosses.
- **The ending.** Built on 2026-10-03 as a choice over the Last
  Wanderer's body: return the Ember, hold the dark as the new dam, or keep
  walking. The first two end the run with a page that names who in
  Emberhold survived; keep walking goes on into the endless Abyss.
- **The Cult's founder.** A name, a line for a villager, maybe a champion
  Cultist in the Abyss.
- **The Oracle's name.** Keep it withheld until something earns it.
- **Daylight.** There is none in the game. The lore can say the Dark has
  eaten the sun since the Theft, or simply never mention it. Leaning: never
  mention it; "moonlight" on the Fields is enough.

## Rebase status

The first pass of in-game text is done. Each item is a text edit only;
none changes mechanics or the balance numbers.

Done on 2026-10-02:

- Voss's opening adds one line after "Wanderer": "The Ember keeps the
  dark out. The dark wants it back."

  uncle lost to the Ember Cult, and Mirela saying the Oracle is holding
  something down.
